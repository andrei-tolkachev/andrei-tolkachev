## Featured project · LoraAI

**LoraAI** is my independently developed, local-first AI platform. Its goal is to bring multimodal understanding, voice interaction, tools, memory, and production workflows together in one system — without making cloud inference a requirement.

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Multimodal inference** | Local language, audio and vision models, including native Qwen3-Omni integration with streaming speech. |
| **Voice & video interaction** | Real-time-feeling conversation, streaming text/audio, and video-call integration. Audio conversation and video-call operation have been tested in my LoraAI environment. |
| **Apps & agents** | Desktop/mobile experiences, tool integrations and a growing local agent workflow. |
| **Creative production** | AI-assisted video editing, DaVinci Resolve automation and production-oriented tools. |
| **Memory & control** | Context continuity, controlled runtimes and private infrastructure — areas of ongoing development and validation. |

**Status:** LoraAI is actively developed; components are at different stages of maturity. The complete platform, private services and production infrastructure **are not open-sourced here**. Selected independent modules and developer tools are published below.
